# fsociety Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: September 25, 2026**

Thank you for choosing **fsociety**, an iOS application for movie enthusiasts to keep track of films they have watched, create movie diary entries, rate movies, write reviews or fan theories, and read content shared by other users.

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use fsociety, how that information is used, and the choices available to you.

By downloading, installing, or using fsociety, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, please discontinue use of the App.

## 1. Information We Collect

### a. Account Information

If you create an account in fsociety, the App may collect information that you voluntarily provide for the purpose of creating and maintaining your account.

Depending on the features available in the current version of the App, this information may include information such as a username or other account-related information.

fsociety does not intentionally request sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for a feature clearly presented to the user.

### b. Movie Diary Information

When you use the movie diary features of fsociety, information you provide may be stored on our servers. This may include:

- Movies you have watched;
- Movie ratings;
- Diary entries;
- Notes about movies;
- Reviews;
- Fan theories;
- Dates associated with diary entries; and
- Other movie-related content that you voluntarily submit.

This information is collected so that fsociety can provide its movie diary and community features.

### c. User-Generated Content

If you submit reviews, comments, fan theories, or other content that is intended to be visible to other users, that information may be publicly displayed within fsociety.

Please do not include sensitive personal information in content that you choose to make publicly visible.

### d. Device or Installation Identifier

fsociety may use an application-specific or installation-specific identifier to associate movie diary information with a particular installation of the App or user account.

This identifier is used for application functionality, such as retrieving and managing your saved movie diary entries. It is not intended to identify you personally outside of fsociety.

### e. Technical Information

When the App communicates with our web server, standard technical information may be processed automatically by the server. Depending on the server configuration, this may include information such as:

- IP address;
- Date and time of a server request;
- Requested API endpoint;
- Device or operating system information transmitted as part of a network request; and
- Technical error or diagnostic information.

This information may be used for security, troubleshooting, fraud prevention, server administration, and maintaining the reliability of the service.

## 2. How We Use Information

Information collected through fsociety may be used to:

- Provide the core functionality of the App;
- Create and maintain user accounts where applicable;
- Save and retrieve movie diary entries;
- Save movie ratings, reviews, notes, and fan theories;
- Display user-generated content where the user chooses to publish it;
- Maintain and improve the functionality and reliability of fsociety;
- Diagnose technical problems;
- Protect the App and its backend services from abuse or unauthorized access;
- Respond to user inquiries or support requests; and
- Comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell users' personal information.

## 3. Advertising and Tracking

fsociety does not use user information for third-party targeted advertising.

fsociety does not intentionally track users across other companies' applications or websites for advertising purposes.

If advertising, analytics, tracking technologies, or additional third-party services are introduced in a future version of fsociety, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

## 4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

### a. Service Providers

fsociety relies on hosting and internet infrastructure to operate its backend services.

Information necessary to provide the service may therefore be processed by infrastructure or hosting providers that operate the servers used by fsociety.

Such providers are used only as necessary to operate, secure, and maintain the service.

Where third parties process user information on our behalf, we expect them to provide appropriate protection for that information.

### b. Public User Content

Content that you intentionally publish as a public review, fan theory, or other public contribution may be visible to other fsociety users.

Information entered into private movie diary features is not intentionally made public unless the App clearly indicates otherwise.

### c. Legal Requirements

We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable laws, regulations, legal processes, or valid governmental requests.

Information may also be disclosed when reasonably necessary to protect the rights, property, security, or safety of fsociety, its users, or others.

## 5. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide fsociety's services, maintain the integrity and security of the service, comply with applicable legal requirements, or resolve disputes.

Movie diary entries, reviews, ratings, and other user-created information may remain stored until:

- You delete the relevant content using functionality provided in the App;
- You request deletion of your information;
- Your account is deleted, where account deletion is available; or
- The information is no longer required to operate the service.

Some limited information may remain temporarily in server backups or logs after deletion as part of normal technical operations.

## 6. Data Deletion and Privacy Choices

Users may delete supported movie diary entries or other content through deletion features available within fsociety.

If you would like to request deletion of information associated with your use of fsociety, you may contact us using the method provided in the **Contact Us** section below.

When submitting a deletion request, we may need sufficient information to identify the applicable account or application data.

We will make reasonable efforts to process valid deletion requests in accordance with applicable law.

## 7. Security

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures designed to protect information processed through fsociety against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

Communication between the App and its backend services may use secure network connections where supported.

However, no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Therefore, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 8. Children's Privacy

fsociety is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children below the minimum age permitted by applicable law without appropriate authorization.

If we become aware that information from a child has been collected in circumstances where parental or guardian consent is legally required, we will take reasonable steps to delete that information.

## 9. Third-Party Content and Services

fsociety may display movie information, images, links, or other content obtained from or associated with third-party services.

Third-party websites and services have their own privacy practices. This Privacy Policy applies only to information processed by fsociety and does not govern independent third-party websites or services.

## 10. International Data Processing

Because internet hosting and infrastructure providers may operate in different countries, information processed through fsociety may be stored or processed outside the country in which you reside.

Where required by applicable law, reasonable safeguards will be used for international transfers of personal information.

## 11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect:

- Changes to fsociety features;
- Changes to our data-handling practices;
- Technical or security changes;
- Changes to third-party services; or
- Legal or regulatory requirements.

When this Privacy Policy is updated, the revised version will include a new effective date.

We encourage users to review this Privacy Policy periodically.

## 12. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or the handling of information through fsociety, please contact the developer through the contact information provided on the fsociety App Store listing or the official fsociety support page.

You may also use this contact method to request access to or deletion of information associated with your use of the App.

Feedback about fsociety is also welcome through the Apple App Store.

If you enjoy using fsociety, you are welcome to leave a rating or review on the App Store.
